Privacy Policy — MatchTrak Helper

Effective date: May 14, 2026

No data collection

MatchTrak Helper does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All information processed by the extension stays on your device.

Local storage

The extension uses chrome.storage.local to cache parsed game data on your device. This cache reduces redundant network requests to matchtrak.com and is never sent anywhere. You can clear it at any time using the Refresh button in the extension, or by clearing your browser's extension storage.

Network requests

The extension fetches pages from matchtrak.com to build the Lifetime Stats table. No requests are made to any third-party server. No analytics, tracking, or telemetry of any kind is included.

Permissions

The extension requests two permissions:

  • Host access to matchtrak.com — required to read and fetch referee profile pages.
  • storage — used solely for the local cache described above.
